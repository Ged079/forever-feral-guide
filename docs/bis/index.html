How to gear

    Rules of thumb

      Method

      The set was found by a search in WoWSims Forever: from the sim's preset gear, every shortlisted item in every slot was swapped in one at a time and the best swap kept, as long as it still won on fresh random runs, until no swap helped. Then every pair of slots was tried, then the weapon enchant.

      The ranking on this page then simmed each slot's options against that set, 3,000 iterations each on the same seed, so the gaps inside a slot are measured precisely. Cat build, 3 minute boss, full raid buffs and consumables.

      Shortlist. Phase 1 cloth and leather, druid weapons and idols, level 60 or lower. Left out: raid drops (Molten Core, Onyxia, ZG, BWL, AQ, Naxx), raid reputation (The Watchers), the Onyxia head quests and PvP rank gear. Each slot's shortlist is the top 30 by stat weights plus up to 10 items with use effects or set bonuses (45 weapons), so a weak-looking item with a strong proc still gets simmed.

      "No AV, PvP or exalted rep" swaps every piece that needs Alterac Valley, PvP or exalted reputation for the best item in that slot that doesn't, and estimates the set's DPS by adding the gaps. Gaps between slots aren't always independent, so treat that number as close, not exact.

      Not counted: bonuses that need a zone or creature type, and gaps under about 1 DPS, which are ties.
