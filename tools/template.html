                    Macros

                    How we tested

                    How the builds were found. Starting from each of the sim's presets, every legal way to move 1, 2, 3 or 5 points from one talent to another (row requirements, prerequisites, 51 points) was simmed at 1,000 iterations; the best move was kept only if it still won at 6,000 iterations on fresh random runs, and the climb repeated until no move helped. Hybrid had to keep Thick Hide, Lacerate and Primal Bite. Two more Cat searches started from scrambled builds: The gear search then ran on the found Cat build, and both builds were searched again on the new gear; neither moved.

                    Rotation. Every number in the sim's Adaptive Cat rotation (Shifting Power threshold, Rip and Bite combo points, Bite energy targets and timings) was swept for both builds, along with reorderings (Rip before Bite, Rake before Rip, Shifting Power earlier or later, no Bite, no Rake). The sim's values were already the best; the one gain, skipping Innervate, only holds on fights of 3 minutes or less.

                    A talent's, consumable's or buff's worth is the build's DPS minus the same build without it, on the same seed. Races are one sim each in the same gear. Stat weights add and remove a small amount of each stat (20,000 iterations each) and divide the gap.

                    Rerun on seeds with no iterations in common, every figure here moved by about 0.5 DPS or less, so treat gaps under 1 DPS as ties.

                    Least certain

                    • Gear was searched for the Cat build only; Hybrid wears the same set. Items that need a reputation or profession you don't have are in the list as found; check the runner-up column.
                    • Bonuses that need a zone or creature type (like the Rune of the Guard Captain's) don't count on the test boss.
                    • Rend and Tear assumes the boss is bleeding. Your own Rake and Rip cover that: with no other bleeder you lose DPS.
